#### About "the shape of time"
## 1. What is your core visual concept?
 The core visual revolves around bubbles, used as a metaphor for the fragility and fleeting nature of time.
## 2. Why did you choose these colors, fonts, or composition?
I chose to let the bubbles break out of the frame to represent the unstoppable flow of time that cannot be confined. For the typography, I used orange-red hues, evoking the melancholy and nostalgia often associated with time, much like the fleeting colors of a sunset.
## 3. If the image could move, how would you want it to express "time"?
If animated, I want the bubbles to dynamically burst out of the frame just like real bubbles, emphasizing the rapid, irreversible passage of time through high-speed movement and sudden dissolution.
